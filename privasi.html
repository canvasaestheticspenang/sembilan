Dasar Privasi

Sembilan oleh CANVAS Aesthetics, Pulau Pinang, Malaysia. Tarikh berkuat kuasa: 8 November 2026.

Ringkasnya: Sembilan tiada akaun dan tiada pelayan kami. Tetapan, tempat disimpan, log perjalanan dan gambar avatar anda disimpan hanya pada peranti anda. Lokasi anda digunakan secara langsung untuk navigasi dan dihantar sebagai koordinat kepada perkhidmatan pihak ketiga yang disenaraikan di bawah supaya laluan, carian dan peta dapat dipaparkan.

1. Apa yang kami kumpul

CANVAS Aesthetics ("kami") tidak mengumpul, menerima atau menyimpan data peribadi anda di mana-mana pelayan milik kami. Kami tidak mempunyai sistem akaun, tidak menggunakan alat analitik atau iklan, dan tidak menjejak anda.

Aplikasi ini memproses perkara berikut pada peranti anda:

  • Lokasi tepat anda (GPS), hanya semasa aplikasi digunakan, untuk menunjukkan kedudukan anda, mengira laluan dan memberi panduan giliran demi giliran.
  • Tetapan (bahasa, tema, suara, mod penunggang, dll.), tempat yang anda simpan, sejarah carian terkini, log perjalanan, nombor WhatsApp keluarga (jika anda masukkan) dan gambar avatar penunggang (jika anda pilih).
  • Aplikasi menggunakan kunci API TomTom terbina dalam untuk carian dan anggaran trafik; anda boleh menggantikannya dengan kunci anda sendiri (pilihan).

2. Apa yang kekal pada peranti

Semua yang disenaraikan dalam bahagian 1 disimpan dalam storan setempat (localStorage) pelayar atau aplikasi pada peranti anda. Kami tidak dapat melihatnya dan ia tidak dimuat naik kepada kami. Gambar avatar tidak dihantar ke mana-mana; ia hanya dipaparkan dalam aplikasi.

Nombor WhatsApp keluarga dan mod penunggang disimpan secara setempat sahaja. Jika anda memilih untuk menghantar laporan perjalanan atau mesej "selamat tiba", aplikasi membuka pautan wa.me ke WhatsApp dengan mesej yang telah diisi. Anda perlu menekan hantar sendiri. Mesej itu kemudian tertakluk kepada WhatsApp dan dasar privasinya, bukan kami. Kami tidak menghantar apa-apa secara automatik.

Kami tidak menyimpan data anda, jadi tiada apa yang perlu kami padam. Data setempat kekal pada peranti anda sehingga anda memadamnya. Cara memadam:

  • Dalam aplikasi: Tetapan, kemudian padam tempat disimpan, log perjalanan, nombor keluarga atau avatar.
  • Padam semua: nyahpasang aplikasi, atau (untuk versi web) kosongkan data tapak dalam tetapan pelayar anda.
  • Kebenaran lokasi boleh ditarik balik pada bila-bila masa dalam tetapan iOS atau Android.

Perkhidmatan pihak ketiga mungkin menyimpan log permintaan mengikut dasar mereka sendiri; sila rujuk dasar mereka.

Privacy Policy

Sembilan by CANVAS Aesthetics, Penang, Malaysia. Effective date: 8 November 2026.

In short: Sembilan has no accounts and no server of ours. Your settings, saved places, trip logs and avatar photo are stored only on your device. Your location is used live for navigation and is sent as coordinates to the third-party services listed below so that routes, search and maps can work.

1. What we collect

CANVAS Aesthetics ("we") does not collect, receive or store your personal data on any server we own. There is no account system, and we use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

The app processes the following on your device:

  • Your precise location (GPS), only while the app is in use, to show your position, calculate routes and give turn-by-turn guidance.
  • Settings (language, theme, voice, rider mode and so on), places you save, recent searches, trip logs, a family WhatsApp number (if you enter one) and a rider avatar photo (if you choose one).
  • The app uses a built-in TomTom API key for search and traffic estimates; you may replace it with your own key (optional).

2. What stays on your device

Everything listed in section 1 is kept in the local storage (localStorage) of your browser or app on your device. We cannot see it and it is not uploaded to us. The avatar photo is not sent anywhere; it is only displayed in the app.

The family WhatsApp number and rider mode are stored locally only. If you choose to send a trip report or a safe-arrival message, the app opens a wa.me link in WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You must press send yourself. That message is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy, not by us. We never send anything automatically.

3. Third parties that receive data, and why

To work, the app contacts third-party services directly from your device. They receive at least your IP address and the request data, and they have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

4. Retention and deletion

We do not hold your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Local data stays on your device until you remove it. To delete it:

  • In the app: Settings, then delete saved places, trip logs, the family number or the avatar.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or (web version) clear site data in your browser settings.
  • Location permission can be withdrawn at any time in iOS or Android settings.

Third-party services may keep request logs under their own policies; please see their policies.

5. Children

Sembilan is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Because the app is for motorcycle riders, users should be old enough to hold a valid licence. A parent or guardian who finds a child using the app can delete the local data as described in section 4.
