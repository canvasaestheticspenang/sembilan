## 5. Full descriptions

### English
Sembilan is a free navigation app for riders and drivers in Malaysia. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your settings, saved places and trip logs stay on your device.

What it does
- Turn-by-turn voice guidance in Bahasa Malaysia or English, with automatic rerouting.
- Search for places and addresses in Malaysia, Singapore and Brunei.
- Petrol stations, R&R areas, toll plazas and speed cameras along the route.
- Toll fares for PLUS highways, shown as estimates.
- Map styles: cartoon, standard and satellite. Heading-up and 3D views.

Rider mode
- Rain forecast along your route and a suggested best departure time.
- Rain radar and nearby shelter spots.
- Warnings for roads where motorcycles are not allowed.
- Optional: send a trip report or "arrived safely" message to a WhatsApp contact you choose. You press send yourself.

Privacy
Location is used while the app is open, to show your position and calculate routes. To do this the app sends coordinates and search text to third-party map, routing and weather services (listed in the privacy policy). We have no server and receive none of your data.

Safety
Do not use the phone while riding. Use a secure mount and voice guidance. Routes, arrival times, toll fares and warnings are estimates. Road signs and the law always come first.

Data credits: OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenFreeMap, Esri, RainViewer, Open-Meteo, TomTom, Valhalla, OSRM, Photon, Nominatim, Foursquare Open Source Places, PLUS Malaysia.

### Bahasa Malaysia
Sembilan ialah aplikasi navigasi percuma untuk penunggang dan pemandu di Malaysia. Tiada akaun, tiada iklan dan tiada penjejakan. Tetapan, tempat disimpan dan log perjalanan kekal pada peranti anda.

Fungsi
- Panduan suara giliran demi giliran dalam Bahasa Malaysia atau Inggeris, dengan laluan semula automatik.
- Cari tempat dan alamat di Malaysia, Singapura dan Brunei.
- Stesen minyak, kawasan R&R, plaza tol dan kamera laju sepanjang laluan.
- Kadar tol lebuh raya PLUS, dipaparkan sebagai anggaran.
- Gaya peta: kartun, biasa dan satelit. Paparan arah hadapan dan 3D.

Mod penunggang
- Ramalan hujan sepanjang laluan dan cadangan masa bertolak terbaik.
- Radar hujan dan tempat berteduh berdekatan.
- Amaran jalan yang melarang motosikal.
- Pilihan: hantar laporan perjalanan atau mesej "selamat tiba" kepada kenalan WhatsApp pilihan anda. Anda sendiri menekan hantar.

Privasi
Lokasi digunakan semasa aplikasi dibuka, untuk menunjukkan kedudukan dan mengira laluan. Untuk itu, aplikasi menghantar koordinat dan teks carian kepada perkhidmatan peta, penghalaan dan cuaca pihak ketiga (disenaraikan dalam dasar privasi). Kami tiada pelayan dan tidak menerima data anda.

Keselamatan
Jangan guna telefon semasa menunggang. Gunakan pemegang yang kukuh dan panduan suara. Laluan, masa tiba, kadar tol dan amaran adalah anggaran. Papan tanda jalan dan undang-undang sentiasa diutamakan.

Penghargaan data: penyumbang OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap, Esri, RainViewer, Open-Meteo, TomTom, Valhalla, OSRM, Photon, Nominatim, Foursquare Open Source Places, PLUS Malaysia.
